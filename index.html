About

I am an adjunct lecturer and independent researcher working with LLMs. I’m curious about how they can be used more effectively in areas where mistakes matter, like healthcare and accessibility. Right now, I’m studying how vision encoders handle Bangla sign language, as a step toward LLM-based sign language translation. I am also looking into diffusion models and actively trying to find gaps. I like finding and questioning new approaches and seeing how well they hold up.

I’m looking for a PhD position where I can keep building on this work, especially in healthcare and low-resource languages, and I’m open to related directions within NLP.
